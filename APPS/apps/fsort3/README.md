# Evans Griffiths & Hart, Inc. (EG&H) FSORT3 & VSELECT V3.5

**EG&H FSORT3 & VSELECT V3.5** is an authentic DEC BASIC-PLUS-2 recreation of the high-speed multi-key record sorting (`FSORT3`) and boolean record selection/projection (`VSELECT`) utility authored by **Evans Griffiths & Hart, Inc. (Lexington, MA)** for DEC PDP-11 RSTS/E systems (1977–1989).
