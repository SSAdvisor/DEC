# MCBA V7.2 Accounting, Distribution & Manufacturing (MRP) Suite

**MCBA V7.2** is an authentic recreation of the classic DEC OEM modular ERP & Manufacturing system authored by **Mini-Computer Business Applications, Inc. (MCBA)** for DEC PDP-11 RSTS/E and CTS-300/500 systems (1975–1992), including `COP`, `I/M`, `BOMP`, `MRP`, `SFC`, and `G/L`.
