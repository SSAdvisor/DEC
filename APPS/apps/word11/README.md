# WORD-11 V3.1 (Data Processing Design, Inc.)

**WORD-11** was the premier third-party multi-terminal word processing and office automation system for **DEC PDP-11 RSTS/E** (and later VAX/VMS), developed by **Data Processing Design, Inc. (DPD)** of Anaheim, California.

## Key Capabilities
- **Document Library (`DIR`, `SELECT`, `CREATE`, `READ`, `COPY`, `DELETE`, `PURGE`)**: Manages documents across `ACTIVE`, `FINAL`, `SPOOL`, and `WASTEBASKET` status categories.
- **VT100 `GOLD` Key Macros (`GOLD`, `GOLD C`, `GOLD B`, `GOLD S`, `GOLD P`)**: Emulates DPD WORD-11's famous `PF1 (GOLD)` keypad operations (centering headings, bolding titles, spell-checking, and spooling to `LP0:`).
- **Spelling Checker & Dictionary (`SPELL`, `DICT`, `DICTADD`)**: Scans documents against the WORD-11 corporate/technical lexicon and reports unknown words or verifies spelling accuracy.
- **List Processing & Mail-Merge (`LPLIST`, `LPADD`, `MERGE`)**: Substitutes `<NAME>`, `<COMPANY>`, and `<SYSTEM>` fields across form letters.
- **Ruler, Pagination & Spooling (`RULER`, `HEADER`, `FOOTER`, `FORMAT`, `SPOOL`, `PRINT ON`, `EXPORT`)**.
