# Collier-Jackson, Inc. CJ/BUSINESS V5.4 (CJ/GL, CJ/PAYROLL, CJ/CIRC)

**Collier-Jackson CJ/BUSINESS V5.4** is an authentic DEC BASIC-PLUS-2 recreation of the enterprise general ledger (`CJ/GL`), human resources/payroll (`CJ/PAYROLL`), and newspaper circulation & advertising billing (`CJ/CIRC`, `CJ/AD`) suite authored by **Collier-Jackson, Inc. (Tampa, FL)** for DEC PDP-11 RSTS/E and VAX/VMS systems (1977–1989).
