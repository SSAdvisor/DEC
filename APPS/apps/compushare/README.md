# Compu-Share, Inc. CAS V6.1 Accounting & Oil/Gas JIB Suite

**Compu-Share CAS V6.1** is an authentic DEC BASIC-PLUS-2 recreation of the RMS-11 accounting, wholesale distribution, and Oil & Gas Joint-Interest Billing (`JIB`) / Revenue Distribution (`REVDIST`) system authored by **Compu-Share, Inc. (Lubbock, TX)** for DEC PDP-11 RSTS/E and VAX/VMS systems (1978–1989).
