        DIGITAL EQUIPMENT CORPORATION - PDP-11 / VAX SYSTEMS
        ----------------------------------------------------
The EDT text editor was designed by Digital Equipment Corporation as
the standard interactive screen and line editor for DEC operating systems,
including VMS, RSX-11M, and RSTS/E.

Key Features of EDT:
1. Two distinct editing modes:
   - Line editing mode (the asterisk '*' prompt)
   - Change mode (full-screen VT100 keypad editing)
2. Powerful string manipulation and pattern search.
3. Resequencing and multi-line block deletions and insertions.
4. Seamless integration with DEC DCL command procedures.

Welcome to the authentic DEC BASIC-PLUS-2 EDT implementation!
